Case Study

Area

Understanding where translations differ—and how.

Evaluation gap

Standard automatic and human evaluation can miss quality differences specific to EN–JA literary translation. Comparing systems required an explicit set of analytical criteria.

Design decision

Built on MQM/SCATE to define 40 evaluation items covering Japanese and literary features, then analyzed outputs from multiple MT systems and LLMs under consistent conditions.

Analysis

Combined quantitative evaluation with qualitative analysis of translations to characterize system strengths and weaknesses beyond an overall assessment.
